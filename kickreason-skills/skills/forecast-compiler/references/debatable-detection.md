# Detecting "important" and "debatable"

The product is the important + debatable nodes. Both filters are operational, not vibes.

## Important — a 4-gate test (the first two are the hard ones)
1. **Leverage** — flip the answer across its plausible range; does the parent forecast/headline change
   materially? Score 0 if unchanged. (Value-of-Information / sensitivity / a "tornado diagram".)
2. **Uncertain** — is the answer genuinely in doubt now (away from 0/1, ideally near 50/50)? A near-known
   answer carries ~zero information however big the topic sounds. (Brier's "uncertainty" term p(1−p) peaks at 50/50.)
3. **Resolvable** — will it have a clean, dated, undisputed answer so the lean can be graded? (Tetlock's
   Clairvoyance Test.)
4. **Addressable** — can deep research actually move your view? An irreducible coin-flip (a shootout) is
   important to the outcome but not worth researching.

> Bad node: "Will Brazil win and play well?" (two questions in one). Good node: "Will Casemiro start the QF
> given his yellow-card suspension risk?" (one question, specific actor, clean dated answer, swings the model).

## Debatable — three detectors must agree
- **Hedge-without-resolving** — the trace itself hedges and never settles.
- **Claim-vs-negation dispersion** — retrieving the claim AND its negation pulls comparably-credible sources
  (dispersed, not peaked).
- **Market divergence** — the trace's lean diverges from the de-vigged market price.

Drop settled / low-debate nodes. The debate is the value signal.

## Honest caveats
- "Debatable" is Goodhart-gameable → pre-register the selection rule; grade calibration over ALL flagged nodes.
- Source-disagreement can be bad retrieval, not real contest (e.g. the wrong event mixed in) — verify sources.
