The honest part: a 70% call is supposed to lose ~30% of the time — so we never score ourselves on a naïve win/loss tally. We grade calibration (do our 70%s happen ~70% of the time?) and Closing-Line Value (were we early and right?) over a locked, pre-registered set of calls. A single call resolving against us is not a miss. That discipline — not the code — is what makes the scoreboard real.
