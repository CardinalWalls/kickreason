## 2. The common threads

Testing the hypothesis honestly across all ten profiles. It largely **CONFIRMS**, but with two important refinements.

**Thread 1 — "Look RIGHT, never look WRONG/fooled" recurs across all 10.** It is the literal core of every profile's *look-like* and *fears* columns:
- Fans want to be "the one who called it," fear "the loud-wrong-guy." Bettors fear "looking like a mug." Fantasy players have the verbatim "I'll feel stupid if he goes off on my bench." Creators live in receipts/dunking culture. Pundits fear the viral "wrong pundit" clip. CMOs are sold against the literal question *"Have you backed a real winner?"* Coaches want a "defensible paper trail" and to be "proven RIGHT." FIFA fears its superlatives "being challenged as inflated." MiroMind's whole identity is "we can prove every call."
- **Not one stakeholder is indifferent to being seen as right.** This is the strongest single finding.

**Thread 2 — "Show the work / proof, not vibes."** Recurring want for *sourced reasoning + a verifiable track record*, distinct from wanting the answer itself:
- Fans explicitly want "the reasoning shown so they can repeat it and not get clowned" (the differentiator vs the free bare number). Bettors want "un-cherry-picked track records." Creators want "the trace as proof of rigor." Coaches want "show your work" dossiers. Sponsors want a "ledger." FIFA wants "auditable proof, not bare assertion." This is the want incumbents *don't* serve — bare predictions are everywhere; **graded, sourced, accountable ones are scarce.**

**Thread 3 — "Don't get fooled by a faker."** A trust/verification gap recurs sharply in the money-rich middle:
- Bettors (faked tout screenshots, PortmanTracker). Creators (cappers presumed-fraudulent; verification hubs exist *because* nobody trusts self-reported records). Tip-buyers (can't verify before paying). This is a *demand for an external grader* — exactly what self-grading manufactures.

**Thread 4 (refinement, not universal) — "Be on the winning side / culturally central."** Strong for fans (BIRGing), neutrals, sponsors (the moment), FIFA (legacy), but weaker for sharps and trading desks who care about calibration over which side wins.

**Where the hypothesis BREAKS / needs honesty:** the want is intense and universal, but for the mass consumer tiers (fans, casual bettors, casual fantasy/DFS, creators' audiences) **the proof-of-being-right layer is today met by FREE products** — so willingness-to-pay for *that specific layer* is low even where adjacent spend is enormous. Money concentrates in B2B/professional tiers (books, sponsors, broadcasters, clubs, FIFA) and in high-rollers/sharps. The hypothesis is true about *demand*; it is only partly true about *paid* demand.

> **The single deepest shared want: to possess checkable proof that you were right (and your rival/the expert was wrong) — to convert a claim into a receipt.** Everyone makes calls publicly; almost no one can prove their call was good. The instrument that settles "who was right," on the record, sourced, is what every stakeholder is reaching for under different surface goals.

## 3. The demand hotspots — where the want is loudest AND money flows

Ranked by **intensity of want × money already flowing to satisfy it.**

**#1 — Sponsors / brands (CMOs).** Intensity: career-level. Money: nine-figure rights + a crowded paid measurement market (YouGov/Kantar/Zoomph) they *already buy*. The want "look right is sold to them verbatim" ("Have you backed a real winner?"). They want to SEE: a forward "which team/player/narrative will matter" call they can pre-stage creative against, then a timestamped ledger proving the bet paid off. They'd value an instrument that gives a **defensible, third-party-style, dated thesis + a graded scorecard for the board** — the thing that turns "we got lucky" into "we called it, here's the sourced reasoning."

**#2 — Sportsbooks / operators.** Intensity: most acute spend window of the cycle ($3.3–4.3B handle), sharpened by a real trust threat (Kalshi/Polymarket in CA/TX). Money: flows freely (CPA, RevShare, data feeds, boosts). They want to SEE: a credible **"true-odds" benchmark for low-data debutant teams** (risk desk), and a **show-its-work prediction layer that makes their app the authority fans visit** (content/marketing budget — faster to access than the trading desk). Verified that insight-enriched markets lift behavior (+31% bets, ~2x stake).

**#3 — Media / broadcasters.** Intensity: highest-fear, highest-status part of the job (the viral wrong-pundit clip). Money: $1.25B US rights + already-funded Opta/Stats Perform line items for exactly this category. They want to SEE: pre-match talking-point packs + win-probability graphics (which they already pay for) **PLUS the white space — a self-grading "who-was-right" scorecard** they can victory-lap and let fans play against (pundit vs model vs fan), monetizable as 2nd-screen inventory.

**#4 — Clubs / federations / coaches.** Intensity: emotionally charged (Southampton risked a £200M+ expulsion to spy on rivals). Money: growing analytics market ($2.29B→$4.75B), multi-vendor stacks. They want to SEE: opponent dossiers + **a graded "is this run REAL or a small-sample spike" verdict** delivered as clean PDFs/presentations (their stated preferred format) — a backside-covering paper trail against the "short-run-of-form" overpay.

## 5. The wants we CANNOT serve well (honest, one line each)

- **Fans' / casual bettors' / creators' bare "who wins" number** — already given away free by FotMob, Sofascore, Yahoo and FIFA pick'em, so WTP for the prediction itself is ~$0.
- **Diaspora fans' real fears** — ICE/immigration enforcement and ticket affordability are heavier, real-world barriers a forecast product simply does not touch.
- **Actually winning money / a betting edge** — this is a credibility/proof instrument, not a +EV or speed tool; promising profit would be a tout claim and is off-limits.
- **Sponsor ROI attribution** — proving a dollar lift from an activation is YouGov/Kantar's job; we offer defensibility, not attribution, and can't replace that stack.
- **Player load / injury-risk monitoring** — that's GPS-vest hardware (Catapult/STATSports) and proprietary biometric data, outside a deep-research forecast's reach.
- **Live in-play / micro-betting speed** — a ~4-min deep-research run is structurally too slow for second-by-second markets; we are pre-match/pre-decision, not in-play.
- **FIFA HQ's own "most-watched ever" superlatives** — FIFA controls the audience number it would be graded on, so self-grading adds little credibility there (host-city economics and integrity risk are the checkable parts).
- **Real-time fixing/manipulation detection** — integrity surveillance is a specialist live-monitoring function (Sportradar/IC360); we can flag pre-kickoff risk, not catch in-match spot-fixing.
- **Settling tip-buyers' trust at scale cheaply** — the want is real, but this cohort is conditioned to free/low-price and presumed fraud, so converting them is a hard, low-margin sell.

*Position throughout: a credibility/proof instrument that manufactures checkable receipts of who was right — never a speed, edge, or betting-tip claim.*
