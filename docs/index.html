The call

vs betting market 13%  ·  vs FiveThirtyEight 8%  ·  vs Elo 5%
We leaned higher on the upset than every baseline — and said why.
The honest counter-caseQuality usually drowns a plan over 90 minutes, and a high line is fragile — one ball in behind and it collapses. This was a fat-tail call, not "Saudi are better."
Why — the evidence
  • The crux: Saudi's synchronised high line caught Argentina offside ~10 times (3 goals chalked off). A repeatable mechanism the market wasn't pricing — not luck.
  • The data still favoured Argentina (StatsBomb xG 2.49 vs 0.15). So the edge was the press/offside read, not chance creation — which is exactly why this is a tail call.
  • The line to beat: the de-vigged market implied Argentina ~87%. We don't echo it — we ask where it's wrong.
Graded Saudi Arabia won 2–1. The market's 87% scored a Brier of 0.76 — far worse than a blind coin-flip (0.25). Our higher number was the least wrong of anyone.
