## 1. The view-layer spine (expanded)

We started with four layers (odds, stats, narrative, magic-moment). The research confirmed all four are real professions with real systems — and surfaced **more layers** the industry visibly serves but our original model missed. Two of the new wedge-slices are the most important strategic additions: **ACTIONABLE-PICKS** (the most downstream layer, where odds+stats+narrative collapse into one deadline move and then self-score in points and money) and **CALIBRATION/TRUST** (the layer **no incumbent ships** — our wedge).

| # | Final layer | One-line definition | Profession | Buyer |
|---|-------------|---------------------|------------|-------|
| 1 | **ODDS** | The calibrated probability of a result, expressed as a tradeable price (and, downstream, the consumer forecast/bracket) | Odds compiler / trader / quant / syndicate modeller; tipster/SEO operator (consumer) | Bettors, sharps, quants, books; casual fans (consumer) [1][2][10] |
| 2 | **STATS** | The underlying data + pattern the forecast is built on (xG, tracking, OBV, xT) | Data scout, data scientist, performance analyst | Clubs, analysts, B2B feed buyers [3] |
| 3 | **NARRATIVE** | The storyline / the "why" behind the forecast | Tactical writer, video essayist, data journalist, pundit | Media, creators, fans [6] |
| 4 | **MAGIC-MOMENT** | The star / drama / turning point surfaced and sold as attention | Social producer, clipper, highlights editor; match-data operator | Fans, social, broadcasters, sponsors [7] |
| 5 | **ACTIONABLE-PICKS (fantasy / DFS / props)** *(new wedge)* | Collapses a probability, a pattern and a why into **one move before a deadline** (captain X, start Y over Z, take the over on prop P) — then scores itself in points and money | Fantasy projections modeler, DFS pro, lineup/sim engineer, props handicapper, FPL "Scout" | Recreational FPL/DFS player, semi-pro grinder, props/pick'em bettor, operators (B2B) [11] |

| 7 | **INTEGRITY / RISK** *(new)* | "Is this market clean, or is the result for sale?" — P(manipulation) + a graded risk dossier | Integrity analyst, intelligence investigator | Federations, regulators, books, law enforcement [8] |
| 8 | **MOMENT-DETECTION / EVENT-TRUTH** *(sub-layer)* | The authoritative call of *when/whether* a moment happened (goal, offside, scorer) | Officiating-tech operator, match-data operator | FIFA/referees, then betting settlement [7] |
| 9 | **CALIBRATION / TRUST (track-record)** *(new — OUR WEDGE)* | "How right has this forecaster actually been?" — a published, properly-scored, checkable accuracy record | Superforecaster, calibration scientist, benchmark maintainer; independent proofer | Trust-seeking fans, quants, journalists, B2B buyers; governments/funds (at the science end) [9][12][13] |

**Why the new layers matter to us.** Layer 9 (Calibration/Trust) is the one **no incumbent ships** and the one MiroMind's verified self-grading is built to own — it is literally the empty box KickOracle asks buyers to trust on faith [9][10]. Two adjacent slices reinforce it from opposite ends: the **tipster-tout economy** [13] is an entire market that runs on the single broken promise "trust my track record" and is full of mechanisms to fake it; the **forecasting-calibration science** [12] is the mature discipline (Brier, log-loss, calibration curves, strictly-proper scoring) that grades forecasters honestly — the discipline MiroFlow's #1 FutureX ranking was minted by. Layer 5 (Actionable-Picks) is the most commercially proven downstream layer and the natural cross-sell target of one trace. Layers 6-8 are adjacent B2B markets the *same* trace can be re-read into.
